"Bitcoin to Zero" Searches Hit All-Time High

October 19, 2026 · PixelForge Trends Desk
₿

Searches asking whether Bitcoin is going to zero just hit an all-time high. What the panic — and the data — actually say.

What's happening

Search interest in the phrase “Bitcoin to zero” has climbed to an all-time high, a classic signal that fear is doing the rounds among crypto watchers.

Why everyone's talking about it

Bitcoin has a long history of surviving exactly these moments — every major drawdown brings a fresh wave of obituaries, and every recovery makes them look premature. The search spike itself has become the story: it measures sentiment better than it predicts price.

What to watch next

Watch whether the fear fades or feeds on itself. Historically, peak panic-search moments have been noisy but not prophetic — the chart, not the chatter, gets the final word.
